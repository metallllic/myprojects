Import from Books app

  1. Open the Books app and go to Library.
  2. Long-press the book, then tap Share.
  3. Tap Save to Files and save it to iCloud Drive or On My iPhone.
  4. Come back here, tap Choose book and pick the saved file.

No Share option? Open the Files app → iCloud Drive → Books; books you added yourself are often already there. Books bought from the Apple Book Store are copy-protected and can’t be imported.
